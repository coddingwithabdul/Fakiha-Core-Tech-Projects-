ABOUT SKILLSWAP

Building a Campus Community of Learners

SkillSwap Campus is a student-focused platform designed to make knowledge sharing easier. We believe students don't have to learn everything alone. By connecting with peers, students can exchange practical skills, collaborate on projects and build meaningful connections.

  • Share your expertise
